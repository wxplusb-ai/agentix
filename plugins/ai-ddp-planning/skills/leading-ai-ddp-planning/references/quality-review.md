# Quality Review

What a sound deliverable looks like at each step. The Team Lead checks every deliverable against these criteria before the client sees it. The criteria describe outcomes, not procedures: judge whether the deliverable achieves them.

## Contents

- All deliverables
- 01 Project Overview
- 02 Beliefs and Bets
- 03 Project Scope
- 04 Estimation Discovery
- 05 Project Plan
- 06 ClickUp Publication

## All deliverables

- Frontmatter is complete and the status is correct.
- Written in the client's terms, not catalog jargon. A non-technical sponsor could follow it.
- Every claim about the client's situation traces to something the client said or provided. Inferences are marked as inferences.
- Nothing from the catalog is restated at length; nodes are referred to by number and name.
- Open questions are listed, not buried.

## 01 Project Overview

- Every interview topic is covered, or the gap is stated with a reason.
- Every catalog category is marked in play, out of play or unknown, with a one-line reason. Unknown categories have a follow-up question or a candidate bet.
- For an in-flight project: what is complete, in progress and planned is clear enough that the Architect will not plan finished work again.
- The client's beliefs and risks are recorded in their own words, ready for Step 2.
- An Architect reading only this document could form a first view of which nodes the project needs.

## 02 Beliefs and Bets

- Each belief is a testable statement: it is clear what evidence would confirm or refute it.
- Beliefs cover the whole venture, not only technology: customers, value, data, model capability, cost, operations, adoption, safety.
- Consequence and evidence ratings are the client's, or agreed with the client.
- Bets are ranked, and the ranking would survive the question "why is this one above that one?"
- Leaps of faith are flagged.
- Knowledge is separated from belief: anything with real evidence names the evidence.

## 03 Project Scope

- Every bet maps to at least one node, or is listed as a proposed catalog addition. Nodes do not need a bet: most are there because the application needs them.
- No node duplicates work the overview records as complete.
- Node inputs are produced by an earlier or concurrent node, already exist, or are explicitly external.
- Phases are coherent collections with names that say what each accomplishes, and their order puts evidence for the riskiest bets early.
- Inclusions that are not obvious, and notable exclusions, are explained.
- Proposed catalog additions describe the node well enough to be added: purpose, outputs, the bet it tests and rough tasks.

## 04 Estimation Discovery

- The planning horizon is stated: which nodes will be planned at task level, which at node level, which phases in outline.
- Every task-level node's Discovery for Planning questions, and the Required Information of each of its tasks, are covered.
- Questions are merged where they overlap and phrased in the client's terms.
- Every question has an answer: a value, or a low and high range with the client's reasoning. "Unknown" is not an answer.
- Answers that contradict earlier deliverables are flagged.

## 05 Project Plan

- The Plan sheet has exactly the required columns, in order.
- Task-level rows use catalog tasks, adapted to the project's terms, with estimates that follow from the task's estimation guideline and an answered question.
- Node-level and phase-level rows are presented as approximations with ranges, not as commitments.
- Dates respect the client's stated capacity and the dependencies between nodes.
- Priority (Urgent, High, Normal, Low) is sensible: work on the leaps of faith and hard deadlines is near the top.
- Tags carry each node's catalog category and the IDs of the bets it tests.
- Totals on the Summary sheet agree with the rows.

## 06 ClickUp Publication

- What was published matches the approved workbook: one folder per phase, one list per node, one task per row, with the mapped fields correct.
- After publishing, the record lists every created item with its ClickUp ID, and anything that failed.
- Nothing was published before the client reviewed the plan in Excel and authorized the upload.
